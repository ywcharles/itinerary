# A little planning. A lot of possibility.

A calm travel-journal direction using the existing blue #2274A5, sage #80AB82 and brown #826754. Geist handles the interface; an italic editorial accent gives the headline personality. White backgrounds, generous margins, one main illustration, no autoplay video, no scroll hijacking.

## Story and imagery plan

1. **Possibility:** One landscape illustration of an imagined Mediterranean coastal village. The arch crop and a single floating stop card introduce travel and the product together.
2. **Intention:** A functional agenda and an explicitly illustrative map. Clicking a stop synchronizes both; UI remains real HTML rather than a generated screenshot.
3. **People:** The same plan gains a companion’s idea. Initials keep the focus on shared planning without fabricated customer endorsements.
4. **Discovery:** A sample nearby garden suggestion fits the free slot. The demo is local; live AI is not implemented or represented as working.
5. **Begin:** A short closing statement and create-itinerary link. No extra decorative imagery.

## Motion

Desktop uses a sticky agenda beside three chapters with normal browser scrolling. Chapter intersections update the message and suggestion. Cards enter over 400ms and selected map pins respond to clicks. No perpetual movement. Mobile presents a linear story and the interactive preview below. Reduced-motion preferences disable transitions and smooth scrolling.

## Hero generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: wide 3:2 editorial hero illustration for a travel planning landing page. Imagine a quiet Mediterranean coastal town seen from a hillside, warm white houses and earthy terracotta rooftops, a narrow winding pale footpath through sage green trees down to a tranquil blue bay, three very small travelers exploring together. Tactile gouache and subtle paper grain, sophisticated vintage travel poster art with modern restrained composition, no outlines, clean big shapes and beautiful architectural details. Airy cream-white and very pale blue sky occupies upper third. Muted vivid brand blue #2274A5 water, sage #80AB82 greenery, warm brown #826754 roofs, warm white paper. Soft sunlight, unhurried, optimistic. Full bleed landscape image. No text, letters, typography, UI, borders, logos or watermarks.
